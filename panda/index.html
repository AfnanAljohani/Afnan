The problem

Too much stock is waste. Too little is lost sales.

Panda's store and DC replenishment of short-shelf-life products is driven by uncertain demand. Our scope is Product · Supplier · Store.

Overstock

Markdown · expiry · waste

Excess inventory on fresh SKUs ends in price markdowns, expiry and disposal.

Understock

Stockouts · lost sales

Too little stock leaves empty shelves, lost sales and lower on-shelf availability.
