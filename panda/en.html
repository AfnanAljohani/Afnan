BUSINESS UNDERSTANDING

Fresh food doesn't wait: every day of a wrong order has a cost

Fresh products have a short shelf life, so any forecasting error shows up immediately, either as an empty shelf or as an expired product. The project balances demand, inventory and replenishment using product, supplier and store/DC data.

Understock Stockouts

Empty shelves → lost sales and unhappy customers.

Overstock Waste

Forced markdowns and expiry → margin lost directly to waste.

< 15%Forecast MAPE
≥ 85%Near-expiry recall
−20–25%Waste reduction
−25–30%Stockout reduction

NEW FINDINGS

Three new issues surfaced in Phase 3, corrected with business rules

NEW IN P39,825

Inflated Units_Sold

Units_Sold > 1.5 × units implied by Sales_Value ÷ Effective_Price → corrected to implied units + flag.

NEW IN P3964

Zero inventory, no stockout

On_Hand = 0 but Stockout_Flag = 0 → Stockout_Flag_Prepared = 1 (raw kept).

NEW IN P3128,686

Future-dated records

Dated after the 27 Sep 2026 extraction date → no actual sales yet → excluded.
