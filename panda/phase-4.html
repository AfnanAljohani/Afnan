Phase 4 · Modelling

Building models for stockout and near-expiry risk, demand forecasting, anomaly detection and replenishment policy simulation.

Modelling approach

Four analytical tracks on the same data

Planned. This content is the plan proposed at the end of Phase 2. It will be replaced with actual results when the Phase 4 files are delivered.
Track 1

Classification

  • Target: Stockout_Flag; near-expiry risk
  • Methods: Logistic Regression · Random Forest · XGBoost
  • Evaluation: Recall, PR-AUC, F1
Track 2

Regression & time series

  • Target: Units_Sold at Category × Day / Store × Category × Week
  • Methods: Seasonal naïve baseline · SARIMA / Prophet · LightGBM with lags
  • Evaluation: MAE, RMSE, MAPE (target < 15%) on a time holdout
Track 3

Anomaly detection

  • Target: Units vs sales-value mismatch; waste spikes
  • Methods: Isolation Forest · robust z-score
  • Evaluation: Precision on the 9,825 known inflated records
Track 4

Replenishment simulation

  • Target: Order quantity policies
  • Methods: Forecast-driven vs current rule
  • Evaluation: Waste, OSA and stockouts per policy
Features use only information available before the prediction date.
